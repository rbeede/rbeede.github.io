--- creation_epoch: 1789758588 lastmod_epoch: 1789758588 categories: ['Tech Tricks'] title: Shrink VM Disk Commands --- Note if the virtual disk is bitlocker encrypted you must remove the bitlocker encryption first and then re-establish it as Used Space Only. If the Hypervisor (i.e. VMware) encrypted mode (vTPM) is enabled you do not have to remove it as VMware shrink commands will still work. In most cases you do not have to defrag (inside guest OS) unless your workloads created heavy fragmentation.
  • Delete all snapshots
  • After wiping disk space make sure to power off
  • Defrag RETRIM is not typically supported or required
dism.exe /online /cleanup-image /startcomponentcleanup

sdelete64.exe -z -nobanner C:


# Might be treated as malware, you can use the GUI "Create a restore point" to manage existing and delete
vssadmin delete shadows /for=C: /all /quiet

manage-bde -status

manage-bde -on C: -used

Note: Do not use manage-bde -wipefreespace as that will cause the virtual disk to expand to its full size instead. 

"c:\Program Files\VMware\VMware Workstation\vmware-vdiskmanager.exe" -k YOUR_DISK.vmdk
