Gigabyte B650M DS3H (rev 1.0)

Advanced Mode

Settings

3.5mm jack ports

"HD Audio Controller" means the audio 3.5mm jack ports built into the motherboard. If you don't use those you can disable this to have one less device (and a fewer drivers) in your OS.

iGPU (integrated) HD Audio

If you don't use the iGPU (in preference of a discrete dGPU) you can also disable:
Settings, AMD CBS
  • NBIO (North Bridge)
  • Audio configuration
  • NB Azalia
  • Allows for HDMI or DispayPort audio to monitors with speakers in the iGPU configuration.
